# TeamSQ – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 26, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

TeamSQ respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

## 2. Information We Collect

When you use TeamSQ, we may collect and store:

- Email address, name, and nickname.
- Account identifiers and authentication information.
- Rosters, player names, skill ratings, and saved matches.
- Subscription status and purchase-related information.

## 3. Photos and Images

Player photos and roster logos are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to TeamSQ servers.

Users are responsible for obtaining appropriate permission to use photos and personal information belonging to other individuals.

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account.
- Provide team-balancing functionality.
- Save and manage rosters, players, and matches.
- Manage subscriptions and premium features.
- Maintain application security.
- Provide customer support.

## 5. Third-Party Services

TeamSQ uses the following third-party services:

- [Google Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) – Authentication, database storage, and backend services.
- [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/) – Subscription management and purchase verification.
- [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) – Payment processing and subscription billing.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

TeamSQ does not directly collect or store your payment card details.

## 6. Data Security

We use authentication and access controls designed to protect your personal information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain personal information as necessary to provide our services.

You can request account deletion through the application or by contacting our support email.

Certain information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and transaction-related purposes.

Deleting your TeamSQ account does not automatically cancel an active Apple subscription. You must manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple account settings.

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or request a copy of your personal information.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the email address below.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Users must comply with applicable minimum age and parental consent requirements.

Parents or legal guardians may contact us regarding personal information provided by their children.

## 10. International Data Processing

Information processed by our third-party service providers may be stored or processed in countries other than your country of residence.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be available on this page.
